The "Vector Equilibrium Jitterbug" (often referred to simply as the Jitterbug transformation) is a dynamic geometric process discovered by architect and systems theorist R. Buckminster Fuller. It demonstrates how the Vector Equilibrium (VE)—mathematically known as a cuboctahedron—can symmetrically contract, twist, and collapse into various other foundational structural geometries. The Vector Equilibrium is the only 3D shape where the length of its outer edges matches the exact distance from its center of gravity to any outer vertex. It features 8 triangular faces and 6 square faces. Because all internal and external forces are completely equalized, Fuller described it as the "zero-phase" or the absolute state of energy neutrality and perfect stillness in nature.

Over the past two years, I have paused my Medium blog to focus on revitalizing research projects unrelated to my job in the United States. My goal is to develop independent algorithmic tools in Computational Design and Engineering, covering Structural, Aerospace, Manufacturing, and Mechanical fields, along with Computer Graphics and the Design for Manufacture (DfMA) process in both AEC and non-AEC contexts. The intention is solely for academic and creative publishing purposes of this algorithmic tool as an independent computational design researcher.

While advancements in Agentic workflows and Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) are enhancing many industries, including companies like Speckle, Hypar, Autodesk Forma, OnShape, Motif, Snaptrude,Machina Labs etc to name a few, there remains a need for speculative research in computational design related to Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI).

Lectures and literature reviews from various authors and conference presenters will support independent exploration in this area. While universities and AI chatbot assistants will be relevant majorly , there still be plethora of independent scholars in the computational design research space.

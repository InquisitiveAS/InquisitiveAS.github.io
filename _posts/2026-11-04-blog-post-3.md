In coordination with my Medium blogposts being on halt since last two years, I have been working on revitalizing my research 
projects which is not related to my work on my regular job in the United States. The aim is to develop algorthmic tools
in Computational Design, Engineering domains (Structural, Aerospace, Manufacturing & Mechanical), Computer Graphics and
DfMA(Design to Manufacture) process in AEC & non AEC.

Despite of the advancements in Agentic workflows & Recursive self-improvement (RSI) will augment workflows in all industries
including product based companies like Speckle,Hypar, Giraffe, Motif, Autodesk Forma,OnShape, to name a few, there still needs
speculative scientific research in post AGI(Artifical General Intelligence) & ASI(Artifical Super Intelligence) computational design practices.

Lectures and literature review by authors from Substack, Medium and co presenters from conferences will only be medium for independent 
researchers when AI will do most human work.
